Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need insurance or ID to be seen?

No. Every clinic is open to anyone regardless of insurance status, income, or immigration status. We only ask for basic contact information to schedule follow-up care if needed.

How do I know when a clinic is near me?

Use the zip code finder above, or subscribe to our newsletter for a monthly schedule sent directly to your inbox in English or Spanish.

Can I volunteer if I'm not a medical professional?

Yes — most clinic days need at least as many non-clinical volunteers as clinicians, covering check-in, translation, child-minding, and set-up.

How is my donation used?

Roughly 90 cents of every dollar goes directly to patient care — supplies, equipment maintenance, and clinic-day logistics. The rest covers the small staff who keep the mission running.
